ION Culture

There is a common thread running through everything we create — it is our way of looking at things, seeking answers to the demands of reality. This translates into deploying the most appropriate techniques, in the broad sense of a synthesis of efficiency, economic feasibility and environmental and social sustainability. And without disregarding the specifics of place, space and historical moment.

The guiding principles of ION

1. Pragmatism
Our projects are concrete, technically feasible and conceptually contemporary
2. Expertise
It is proven by the company's long experience and solid reputation, structured to be able to manage the entire creative process
3. Research, innovation, multidisciplinarity
These are strategic resources for the development of any project, and solid pillars of our work
4. Sharing
A continuous flow of information and mutual care allow us to understand problems and needs, staying on the same wavelength as those we work with
5. Speed
Thanks to the wide-ranging galaxy of our network, we are able to identify the key elements around which to build dynamic and efficient project teams
6. People at the centre
At the heart of our work is a deep understanding of the needs and motivations driving each request. Only in this way can the product/system meet expectations of growth, progress and care for people
7. Lightness
We want to shape our projects with the vibrancy of a lightness rich in essential meaning
8. Contemporary language
We grasp current development trends and know how to translate them in modern terms, without setting aside respect for artistic and stylistic traditions
9. Constant commitment
The success of one of our projects or collaborations does not come from improvisation, but from dedication and relationships built up over time
10. Economic consistency
Consistency between financial commitment and final outcome is among the reasons behind the success of our work

ESG Compliance and the UN 2030 Agenda

Our activities have always been aligned with ESG standards and the principles promoted by the UN 2030 Agenda and the United Nations University. These are the same principles we champion with client companies as a strategic asset for their growth. Respect for the environment, a positive social impact and ethical governance are essential, measurable criteria that guide every choice we make.
